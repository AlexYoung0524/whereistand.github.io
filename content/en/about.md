在场 (zài chǎng) means "being there". In English, this blog is called Where I Stand.

Most of what I write here does the same thing: it takes something people usually accept without a second thought and pulls it apart. Hence the motto, Against Easy Answers.

The posts fall roughly into four categories:

- **Wandering Thoughts**: starting from a small thing or a single idea and following it wherever it goes.
- **Overthinking**: observations prompted by something I experienced.
- **Life's Gift**: about people and memories.
- **59° North**: about Sweden and Europe.

Most posts are available in both Chinese and English.
